The `vp-*.woff2` files are web-optimized builds produced by
`tools/build_webfonts.py` from the corresponding source files in `/fonts`.

  <https://ctan.org/pkg/fandol>.
- **VP Assistant WenKai** is derived from LXGW WenKai Medium and is distributed
  under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source and license:
  <https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai>.
- **VP Metric Display** is derived from Milky Han Mono / Milky Han Term CN and
  is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source and license:
  <https://github.com/milky-han/mhm>.

The internal family names are project-specific so that optimized derivatives
do not reuse reserved upstream family names. The fonts are bundled as part of
the application and are not sold separately.
